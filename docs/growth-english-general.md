# Growth English --- General English Roadmap

> A practical English learning system for everyday life, social
> situations, travel, media, and eventually work.
>
> **Goal:** communicate naturally and confidently in real life --- not
> memorize English for exams.

---

# 🎯 Main Goal

The goal is not:

> "I want perfect grammar."

The goal is:

> **"I can understand people, express myself, keep a conversation going,
> and handle everyday situations in English."**

The learning path:

```text
Input
 ↓
Understand
 ↓
Speak
 ↓
Interact
 ↓
Express Ideas
 ↓
Communicate Naturally
```

# 🧠 Learning Rules

## Rule 1 --- Don't Chase Perfect Grammar

If someone understands you:

```text

```

Improve grammar gradually.

---

## Rule 2 --- Learn Sentences, Not Just Words

Instead of:

```text

```

> "I'm exhausted after work."

```text

```

```text
