# Growth English --- Daily & Work English Roadmap

> A practical English learning track for everyday communication and
> software engineering work.
>
> **Goal:** communicate naturally, clearly, and confidently --- not
> speak perfect textbook English.

---

# 🎯 Main Goal

The target is not:

> "I want to know more English grammar."

The target is:

> **"I can understand people, express my ideas, ask questions, explain
> problems, and communicate professionally in English."**

Focus:

```text
Understand
   ↓
Speak
   ↓
Write
   ↓
Explain
   ↓
Discuss
   ↓
Present
   ↓
Communicate professionally
```

---

# LEVEL 1 --- Everyday English

## Challenge 01 --- Introduce Yourself

### Goal

Be able to introduce yourself naturally.

### Practice

Talk for 1--2 minutes about:

- Name
- Where you live
- Job
- What you do
- Hobbies
- What you're learning

### Example Topics

```text
Who are you?
What do you do?
What are you working on?
What do you enjoy?
What are you currently learning?
```

### Success Criteria

Speak for 2 minutes without translating every sentence from Indonesian.

# LEVEL 4 --- Workplace English

## Challenge 17 --- Daily Standup

Practice:

```text
Yesterday:
What did I do?

Today:
What am I going to do?

Blockers:
Is anything blocking me?
```

### Example

> Yesterday, I worked on the authentication API and fixed a bug in the
> refresh token flow. Today, I'll continue working on the integration
> tests. I don't have any blockers at the moment.

---

## Challenge 18 --- Explain What You're Working On

Practice:

```text
I'm currently working on...
The goal is to...
The main challenge is...
So far, I've...
Next, I'm going to...
```

---

## Challenge 19 --- Ask for Clarification

Useful phrases:

```text
Could you clarify that?

Could you explain what you mean by...?

Just to make sure I understand...

Do you mean that...?

Could you give me an example?

What would be the expected behavior?
```

---

## Challenge 20 --- Confirm Requirements

Practice:

```text
So, if I understand correctly...

The expected behavior is...

The API should...

The user should be able to...

Is that correct?
```

---

## Challenge 21 --- Talk About Blockers

Practice:

```text
I'm currently blocked by...

The issue is...

I've tried...

The problem seems to be...

I need some help with...

Could someone take a look at this?
```

---

## Challenge 22 --- Ask for Help

Avoid:

> "I don't understand."

Use:

> "I'm having trouble understanding this part."

> "I've tried A and B, but I'm still getting this error."

> "Could you help me understand what's going wrong?"

# 🧠 Sentence Pattern Practice

Instead of memorizing isolated words, memorize patterns.

### Giving Opinion

```text
I think...
I don't think...
I would suggest...
I'd recommend...
```

### Explaining

```text
The reason is...
This happens because...
The main issue is...
The problem seems to be...
```

### Uncertainty

```text
I'm not sure yet.
I need to investigate this.
It looks like...
My current assumption is...
```

### Clarifying

```text
Just to clarify...
Do you mean...?
Could you give me an example?
What would happen if...?
```

### Decision

```text
I recommend...
I'd go with...
The trade-off is...
The main advantage is...
The main risk is...
```
